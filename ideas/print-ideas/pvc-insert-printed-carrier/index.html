PVC Bumper Insert in a 3D-Printed Carrier

Idea: print a corner or edge piece with a groove for a ready-made flexible PVC pees. The printed part sets the shape; the softer pees forms the contact surface.

Example: PVC 180 insert

The photo shows the original PVC 180 profile with its insert, not a printed holder.

  • PVC pezen — range
  • Insert PVC 180 Zwart — €5.05 per metre, including VAT (listed price, 9 November 2026).
  • PVC 180 Zwart — the matching standard profile. The listed 27 mm is the base width of this complete profile, not the size of the groove needed in our print.

The supplier suggests warming the insert in hot water to make it easier to fit.

What could we print?

A PETG, PCTG or ASA holder with a channel shaped for this insert. Possible applications include a case corner, protective edge or small bumper.

The key is getting the actual cross-section of the PVC insert, which is not dimensioned on the product page. With that, we could make a short test holder in OpenSCAD and see:

  • How easily the insert slides or presses into place.
  • Whether it stays in the groove, including at the ends.
  • Whether it gives the desired soft contact surface.

The idea is saved for further exploration; no model or test piece has been made yet.
