Router Sled

A router sled guides a handheld router over a large wooden slab or panel to flatten its surface. It is useful when a thickness planer is too small for the workpiece.

Designs to explore

DIY 3D Printed Router Sled — Quigley Tool Design

A modular system combining 3D-printed parts and square metal tubing to support a router over a workpiece. The Etsy listing is for a hardware/acrylic package; the seller offers the 3D-print files and printed parts separately.

S.L.E.D. — One Maker Show

The S.L.E.D. (Surface Linear Equalization Device) uses 25 × 25 mm (1-inch) square tubes, printed supports and bearings. The frame can be adapted to different workpiece sizes. Print files and assembly instructions are offered separately from optional hardware.

These are reference designs for a possible future workshop tool, not completed builds.
