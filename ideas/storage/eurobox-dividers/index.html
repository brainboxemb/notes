Eurobox Dividers

A simple way to divide a standard Eurobox into compartments using wooden panels and 3D-printed connecting pieces.

Eurobox dividers — Armadiaboli

The Eurobox dividers model on MakerWorld shows a storage box divided by wooden strips held in place with printed clips and junctions. This keeps the printed parts small while allowing different compartment sizes.

Points to check before making a version: the internal dimensions of the Eurobox, the required divider thickness, and how well the clips grip the sides of the box.

This is a separate alternative to a portable plywood sorting case: here the box already exists, and the added parts create the compartments.
