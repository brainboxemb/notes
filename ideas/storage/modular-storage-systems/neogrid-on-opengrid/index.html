Neogrid on openGrid

Yes — Neogrid can be mounted directly on openGrid. Neogrid has connector variants designed to clip into the openGrid base. This is a supported arrangement described by Hands On Katie, not something that needs a separately designed adapter.

How it works

An openGrid baseplate covers the drawer bottom. Small printed Neogrid X, T, L and I connectors attach to that base and hold upright dividers cut from sheet material. This makes larger compartments without 3D-printing the entire walls.

Katie recommends openGrid for its firmer attachment of the dividers. She also provides variants for a Gridfinity base, which hold a little less firmly but work with existing Gridfinity drawers.

Parts and fitting

  • Base: print openGrid tiles to suit the drawer. openGrid’s basic grid pitch is 28 mm.
  • Connectors: select the openGrid-compatible Neogrid connector type, not the Gridfinity-specific variant. Use X junctions for intersections, T junctions for branches, L corners and I pieces for straight joins.
  • Divider material: thin MDF, uPVC utility board or acrylic. The standard Neogrid connectors target approximately 8.5 mm material thickness; measure your sheet and print a test connector first.
  • Spacing: follow the Neogrid guide for divider cut lengths on an openGrid base. Do not reuse dimensions calculated for a 42 mm Gridfinity layout.

A configurable alternative is NeoGrid 2.0 by BlackjackDuck, which can generate connectors for an openGrid base, a Gridfinity base, or no grid. It is useful when the divider thickness or compartment layout differs from the original files.

Combining with Gridfinity

Gridfinity bins need a compatible openGrid-to-Gridfinity adapter or holder; they do not clip into openGrid in the same way as Neogrid connectors. The pitches differ: 28 mm for openGrid and 42 mm for Gridfinity. Their dimensions align every 84 mm (3 openGrid cells = 2 Gridfinity cells), which can help plan adapter regions.

For example, a drawer could use Neogrid dividers for hand tools and a small adapted Gridfinity area for screws without replacing the openGrid base.

References: Hands On Katie — Neogrid · Hands On Katie — openGrid · NeoGrid 2.0 on MakerWorld.
