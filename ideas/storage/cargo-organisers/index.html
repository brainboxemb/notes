Cargo Organisers (Printable)

Modular cargo floor systems use interlocking tiles and movable stops to keep items from shifting in a car boot or cargo area. TMAT is a commercial reference; Trunk organizer mat and Open CargoGrid are designs that can be 3D-printed.

Systems

TMAT’s launch period is reported by The Shop; the two print-model dates come from the Trunk organizer mat and Open CargoGrid listings. The dates refer to the commercial introduction or model publication, not necessarily to the original design date.

Open CargoGrid

Open CargoGrid uses Python and build123d to generate custom parts. The linked MakerWorld release uses 60 mm units, 13 mm-thick tiles, and optional 10 mm holes for accessories.

It provides more than 100 accessory variants, including edge and corner pieces, ramps, rods, supports for uneven floors and vertical stop blocks. Because the parts are generated from source, layouts and dimensions can be changed rather than relying only on the supplied printable files. It is not an OpenSCAD project.

Trunk organizer mat

The Trunk organizer mat is assembled from individually printed pieces. Its designer describes a 60 mm X-shaped grid, with optional supports beneath the tiles where the underlying cargo surface is uneven.

Printing an entire cargo floor is described by the designer as time-consuming. Compared with Open CargoGrid, the supplied listing focuses more on the printable mat itself than on parametrically generating variants.

Compatibility

The Open CargoGrid designer explicitly describes the default 60 mm configuration as compatible with Trunk organizer mat, while stating that Open CargoGrid is not compatible with commercial TMAT. These statements concern the published models; custom dimensions could change fit.

Status: saved as references; none of the systems has been printed or tested here.
