Delivery costs by retailer

Standard Dutch transport charges and conditions for the listed Siemens retailers.

Updated: 01 Nov 2026

Standard delivery for a new built-in oven within the Netherlands (excluding installation; policies checked on 1 November 2026):

SellerStandard deliveryConditions
CoolblueFreeFull-day delivery; paid narrower time windows.
Art & CraftFreeStandard delivery free for all order values.
Bemmel & KroonFreeBasic threshold delivery; installation extra.
MediaMarktFreeOrders above €50; premium delivery slots cost extra.
SiemensFreeBasic threshold delivery of large appliances.
KeukenloodsFreeMost Dutch addresses; Wadden Islands excluded.
BudgetplanFreeOrders from €150; exceptions for Wadden Islands, Zeeuws-Vlaanderen, upstairs without lift.
123ApparatuurFreeOrders over €100 and large items ship free. For orders up to €100, shipping costs at most €10.

Conditions: Coolblue, Art & Craft, Bemmel & Kroon, MediaMarkt, Siemens, Keukenloods, Budgetplan, 123Apparatuur.

123Apparatuur shipping policy: orders up to €100 have shipping charges of at most €10; orders above €100 and large products have free transport. The studioLine oven therefore qualifies for free delivery, even though the sales price is quoted on request.

Delivery is not installation. Compare installation and old-appliance removal separately when requesting a quote.

This page covers the suppliers on the replacement models page. Verify checkout charges for installation and old-appliance removal separately.
