Outdoor routers

Working overview of outdoor routers to investigate and possible enclosure-related work.

Created: 11 Nov 2026 Updated: 11 Nov 2026

A working overview of outdoor routers worth examining. The aim is to collect candidate devices and useful references in one place before deciding which ones need a deeper comparison or a separate project.

Routers to examine

The list of specific models still needs to be assembled. Add each router under its exact manufacturer and model name, with a link to the product documentation. This will keep revisions and similarly named models separate.

For each candidate, useful details include:

  • Connectivity: mobile standards, SIM options and Ethernet.
  • Power: input voltage, PoE options and typical consumption.
  • Installation: outdoor rating, mounting options and cable connections.
  • Physical design: body dimensions, antenna positions and clearance with antennas folded.
  • References: datasheet, product page and any practical observations.

Related enclosure work

An initial router measurement and enclosure study is already being developed. It considers the router, its mounting arrangement, the folded antenna positions and the space needed around them.

The power bank shown in the reference case is not part of the router assembly. Measurements from that study should stay tied to the specific router model once it has been identified, rather than being applied to other candidates.
