Outdoor routers

Working overview of outdoor mobile routers and possible enclosure work.

Created: 11 Nov 2026 Updated: 11 Nov 2026

A growing overview of outdoor routers worth considering. This is a collection of candidates and references, not yet a selection.

TP-Link TL-MR100-Outdoor / TL-MR110-Outdoor

Image/source: TP-Link — TL-MR100-Outdoor

These are outdoor 4G LTE routers with integrated 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, a nano-SIM slot and a single Ethernet port. The TL-MR110-Outdoor is also listed by retailers including Amazon.

Outdoor ratingIP65; -30°C to +60°CSame
Dimensions226.6 × 100 × 54.7 mmSame
Power12 V DC, standard PoE or passive PoESame options; check passive PoE voltage

Difference: TP-Link explicitly states that the main difference is the sales channel, with very similar hardware and network performance. No meaningful functional difference is apparent from the published specifications. This does not establish that firmware, regional versions or supplied accessories are interchangeable.

Check before powering: TP-Link’s Dutch MR100 specifications list 24 V / 0.5 A passive PoE, while its MR110 product data lists 48 V / 0.375 A passive PoE. Confirm the actual device label, hardware version and included injector instead of assuming these supplies are interchangeable.

Practical limitation: the Ethernet port is limited to 100 Mbps, even though the LTE modem is rated for up to 150 Mbps downlink.

TP-Link Omada ER703WP-4G-Outdoor

Image/source: TP-Link — ER703WP-4G-Outdoor

The Omada ER703WP-4G-Outdoor is a more capable 4G+ outdoor/indoor gateway than the basic TL-MR100/TL-MR110 models.

FeatureER703WP-4G-Outdoor
Mobile network4G+ LTE Cat 6, up to 300 Mbps down / 50 Mbps up
Wi-FiDual-band Wi-Fi 6 (AX3000): 574 Mbps at 2.4 GHz + 2402 Mbps at 5 GHz
Ethernet3 × Gigabit WAN/LAN: 1 × PoE in, 2 × PoE out
SIM / antennas1 × nano-SIM; 2 detachable LTE antennas (Wi-Fi antennas built in)
Outdoor protectionIP55, operating temperature -30 °C to +60 °C
Size100.4 × 38.7 × 174.8 mm (W × D × H, excluding antennas)
Power802.3at/bt PoE input or 12 V / 2 A DC
ManagementStandalone or Omada centralized management; VPN support

Notable differences: compared with the TL-MR100/MR110, it offers LTE Cat 6 instead of Cat 4, dual-band Wi-Fi 6 instead of 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 4, Gigabit ports with two PoE outputs, and Omada management. However, its IP55 rating is lower than the MR100/MR110’s IP65 rating.

Power and enclosure notes: the manufacturer specifies up to 14.5 W without PoE output or 63 W with PoE output. An external DC adapter may not be included, and PoE output depends on how the device is powered. For an enclosure or mount, allow additional space for the two LTE antennas and connected cables; the published body dimensions exclude the antennas.

MikroTik KNOT Gateway LR8 (EU, IP66)

Image/source: MikroTik — KNOT Gateway LR8

Important: two similarly named devices must not be confused.

  • KNOT Gateway LR8 — newer outdoor gateway, product code L14G-2ax-BT5-LR8&EG800K, rated IP66. It is intended for outdoor mounting and supports European LTE bands, including B20 (800 MHz), as well as GNSS/GPS. This is the candidate here.
  • KNOT LR8G kit — older indoor-oriented device, product code RB924iR-2nD-BT5&BG770A&R11e-LR8G, rated IP20. Not suitable for unprotected outdoor mounting and not included as a candidate.

The similarly styled KNOT Gateway HL9 is IP66 but has North American LTE bands (2/4/5/12/13/66) and 915 MHz Wi-Fi HaLow; it is not included as a European outdoor-router candidate.

Limitation: LTE Cat 1 bis is intended for low-bandwidth IoT use; the maximum 10/5 Mbps is substantially slower than the TP-Link routers above.

GPS time and an NTP server

RouterOS v7 provides two useful building blocks:

  • The GPS package supports /system/gps set-system-time=yes to set the router’s clock from received GNSS time.
  • RouterOS provides an NTP server for time clients on a local network.

To verify on this device: whether GNSS-fed local time can be served via NTP without upstream internet, what settings are required, and what NTP accuracy/stratum are achievable. This should not yet be presented as a confirmed PPS-disciplined or precision time server.
