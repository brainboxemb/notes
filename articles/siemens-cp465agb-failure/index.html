Siemens CP465AGB0/03: sudden power failure

An AI-written case study: how ChatGPT can help investigate a Siemens combi-oven that suddenly went black during use.

Created: 01 Nov 2026 Updated: 03 Nov 2026

Why this memo exists

This is also an experiment in how ChatGPT can help analyse a real-world technical problem. Beyond investigating the failed oven, the goal is to see how useful AI is at organising symptoms, finding documentation and similar failures, identifying possible spare parts, proposing a safe diagnostic approach, and comparing repair with replacement.

The article is written entirely with ChatGPT, using the reported symptoms and researched sources. It is intended as an example of AI-assisted analysis and decision support, not an official Siemens document, a confirmed fault diagnosis or professional repair instructions. Findings and suggested steps still require verification, and any internal microwave repair belongs with a qualified technician.

The problem

The Siemens combi-microwave suddenly went completely black during use. The appliance stopped working at the same time, without an error code to work from.
