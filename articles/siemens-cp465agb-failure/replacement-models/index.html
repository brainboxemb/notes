The two iQ500 models are very similar in cooking features: both have 44 L, 900 W, five heating modes, 15 automatic programs, rapid preheat and humidClean. The main verified difference is the appearance/finish and sales channel, not an obvious increase in cooking capability from choosing studioLine. The iQ700 is a genuine functional upgrade (touch screen, connectivity and additional cooking options), but it costs considerably more. Model features and dimensions are taken from the manufacturer’s linked product pages.

Will they fit in place of the CP465AGB0/03?

The existing Siemens CP465AGB0/03 is 455 × 594 × 545 mm (H × W × D), with a 45 cm installation format. Both iQ500 candidates are 25 mm deeper, while the iQ700 CM724G1B2 is 3 mm deeper than the existing unit. These are appliance dimensions, not a guarantee of installation compatibility. Confirm the cabinet’s available depth, rear clearance, ventilation, electrical connection and door movement against the manufacturer’s installation drawing before ordering.

Preferred option: Siemens studioLine CM485AGB1 without steam. Obtain an actual dealer quote from 123Apparatuur or Bemmel & Kroon. Its price cannot be inferred reliably from the regular iQ500’s ~€620–€660 selling price; that is only a secondary reference, not the price basis for the repair decision. The iQ700 is another product family and is not the preferred replacement. Compare repair and replacement only after the studioLine price is known.
