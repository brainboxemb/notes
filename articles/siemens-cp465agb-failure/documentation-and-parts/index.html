International repair references and related Bosch models

Bosch is a useful search route because Bosch and Siemens share the BSH appliance platform. In particular, the Bosch CPA465GB0 is a much closer comparison than an unrelated Bosch oven: like the Siemens CP465AGB0, it is a 36 L, 1000 W, 455 × 594 × 545 mm compact microwave/oven with steam. The Bosch CPA565GS0 is a related stainless-steel variant.

Concrete common component: international parts cross-references list BSH inverter 12021657 for both Siemens CP465AGB0/03 and Bosch CPA465GB0/03. See the French compatible-model list, UK FixPart part description, and official Bosch part listing. This supports a shared microwave inverter part, not identical main-control boards or a common root cause.

Relevance to the completely black display: a technician would first need to establish where the supply to the control electronics is lost. An inverter is responsible for powering the magnetron and may cause other failures, but there is no evidence yet that this inverter caused the oven to go completely dark. Neither an internal fuse nor a control supply fault is currently confirmed.

Safety: the iFixit and Bosch service manuals above are professional technical references, not instructions to open this microwave at home. Hazardous high voltage can remain after disconnection; internal fault finding and component replacement must be handled by a qualified technician.

Search outcome: relevant Bosch sibling models, international repair guides and a shared BSH component have been found. A freely accessible model-specific CP465AGB0/03 repair schematic or service procedure still has not been verified.

Similar problems found online

These reports are references, not confirmed cases of the same defect:

  • Circuits Online — CP465AGB0/05 and E1007. Very similar model family, with discussion of inverter faults and service documentation. Different symptom: an error message was shown; the appliance did not simply go black.
  • Klusidee — Siemens HF77961NL went dead during cooking. Sudden failure, black display, but also a bang and tripped breaker. Different model; those additional events have not been reported here.
  • Gebruikershandleiding — CP465AGB0/06. A light stayed on and touch controls stopped responding. Different symptom.
  • iFixit — Siemens HF25M5L2 completely dead (February 2026). Closer symptom: no front-panel light or response; different model, and the discussion does not establish which component failed or that a repair succeeded.

No publicly documented case has yet been verified that precisely matches CP465AGB0/03 going black during use with the wall socket and breaker already checked.

Are spare parts or control boards available?

Always search using the full E-Nr., including /03. Boards listed for other revisions are not automatically compatible.

  • FixPart CP465AGB0/03 lists BSH 12021657, an electrical module offered for this revision (about €169 when checked). Other suppliers call it a microwave inverter. It is not identified as the defective main control/power board.
  • BSH 12029511 is a power/control module found for other revisions; /03 compatibility not verified.
  • BSH 11035414 is a user-interface module advertised for later revisions; /03 compatibility not verified.
  • The exact /03 main-power/control PCB part number remains unidentified. Ask Siemens or a parts supplier to check the exploded view and original BSH part numbers before any purchase.

A black display alone does not justify replacing any of these modules.
