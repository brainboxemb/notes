Diagnosis and repair

Safe observations, professional diagnostics and repair services for the CP465AGB0/03.

Updated: 01 Nov 2026

Diagnosis: possible next steps

Without opening the appliance

  1. One power reset, if not already done. If there is no smell, visible damage or tripping breaker, disconnect via the accessible plug or circuit breaker for about 10 seconds, then restore power once. This is the reset in the Siemens instruction manual. Do not repeatedly reset a breaker that trips.
  2. Check the remaining signs of life. Does the interior light come on when the door opens? Any fan, beep or display activity? Note these observations without repeatedly trying to heat.
  3. Record the circumstances. Which function was operating (microwave, hot air, grill or steam)? Any pop, smell, or breaker trip at the moment it went black?

Since the breaker and socket were checked, do not start the investigation by repeating those two checks. If the external supply is confirmed and the appliance remains completely dark after one safe reset, professional diagnosis is the next step.

For a qualified technician

A reasonable fault-isolation approach is to check the power entering the appliance, protective devices, the mains input path, internal low-voltage power supply and control/display electronics. If a protective fuse or thermal cut-out has operated, the underlying cause also needs to be established. Verify the actual failed part and its compatibility with revision /03 before ordering it.

These are areas to investigate, not a diagnosis. A faulty inverter, control board, thermal protection or mains filter has not been established from the symptom alone.

Safety: this appliance contains a microwave high-voltage circuit. Siemens explicitly warns not to remove its housing. High-voltage components can remain dangerous even after unplugging. Internal inspection, measurements, bypassing protection and board replacement must be left to a qualified specialist.

How can it be repaired, and what does it cost?

Siemens service

Siemens Netherlands repair service and the Siemens tariff graphic give an out-of-warranty starting price of €149 for a visit/assessment and a repair without replacement parts. The initial diagnosis is included in the starting charge; it is not a second €149 fee.

If parts are required, the tariff is €149 + part costs + additional labour, with €14.90 per six minutes shown in the graphic. Siemens provides a quotation before proceeding.

One detail to confirm when booking: if a repair estimate is declined, is the fee exactly the €149 starting charge? The published pricing graphic does not spell out that case separately.

The published age discount applies only under certain conditions. FD 9902 means production in February 2019, about 7 years and 9 months before this memo. The purchase date is unknown; any discount must be confirmed with Siemens rather than assumed.

Independent repair service

A qualified microwave/appliance repairer is another option. Ask upfront about diagnosis charges, original BSH spare parts, labour, the quotation process, and the charge if the repair is declined. Do not buy a speculative controller board in advance.

Questions still open

  • Has a deliberate power reset been tried since the failure?
  • Does the interior light come on with the door open?
  • Which function was running when the display suddenly went black?
  • Was there a noise, smell, or breaker trip at the moment of failure?
  • Can we obtain the exact /03 power-control PCB part number or a professional service diagram?
