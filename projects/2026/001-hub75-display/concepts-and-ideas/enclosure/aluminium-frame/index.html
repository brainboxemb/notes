Aluminium Frame Design Considerations

The internal frame uses 20 × 20 mm aluminium construction profiles.

Main dimensions

DimensionValue
Overall width840 mm
Overall height360 mm
Horizontal profiles840 mm
Vertical profiles320 mm
Profile size20 × 20 mm

The overall height consists of a 20 mm top profile, a 320 mm vertical profile and a 20 mm bottom profile.

Panel mounting

The five HUB75 panels will be mounted to the frame using custom brackets.

Because the brackets are designed specifically for the display, the profile positions do not need to follow the original panel mounting-hole pattern directly. This allows the frame dimensions to be defined independently from the panel mounting points.

Profile layout

The horizontal profiles extend across the full width of the frame. The vertical profiles are mounted between the upper and lower horizontal profiles.

Why the horizontal profiles remain full width

Keeping the horizontal members at the full 840 mm keeps their ends accessible after assembly.

This makes it possible to add sliding T-nuts later without dismantling the frame. They can be used for:

  • panel mounting brackets;
  • additional supports;
  • electronics mounting;
  • cable-management parts;
  • future modifications.

If the horizontal profiles were mounted between full-height vertical profiles, their ends would be blocked after assembly.

Current design

The current frame therefore uses:

  • 2 × horizontal profile: 840 mm;
  • vertical profiles: 320 mm;
  • profile size: 20 × 20 mm;
  • overall frame size: 840 × 360 mm.
