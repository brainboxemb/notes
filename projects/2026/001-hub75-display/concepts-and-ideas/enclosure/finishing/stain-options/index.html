Wood Stain Options

Options for an opaque exterior wood stain. Reason for considering beits: hopefully less work than priming and painting in separate steps. That is still to be checked against the application instructions for the chosen product and Okoumé plywood. The transparent Hornbach silver-grey stain was excluded.
