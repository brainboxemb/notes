Finishing the Wooden Enclosure

The finish for the HUB75 wooden enclosure is not yet selected. Two approaches are being considered:

  • Wood stain (beits) — the first option to explore, mainly in the hope that it means less preparation and fewer steps. Opaque outdoor stains in RAL 7035 or RAL 7001, with product photos, prices and reviews.
  • Primer & paint (grondverf en lak) — an alternative with a separate primer and exterior topcoat; product selection is still open.

Edge sealing (randsealer) is a separate preparation step to protect the cut plywood edges, regardless of the chosen finish.

Enclosure colour preview

Both colour variants use the same five-panel black LED screen and enclosure shape. RAL 7035 provides more contrast and should reflect more sunlight; RAL 7001 looks subtler and more industrial. This is an indicative render rather than a calibrated colour sample.

Quantity

For the provisional 900 × 450 × 100 mm enclosure: approximately 2.16 m² per coat for both inner and outer surfaces, or 4.32 m² for two coats, before subtracting openings or allowing for wastage.
