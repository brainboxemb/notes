Finishing the Wooden Enclosure

The current preference is a simple silver-grey wood stain.

The existing display enclosures appear to have a black finish. A lighter colour is preferred for the new display because it may be used in direct sunlight.

A silver-grey finish should:

  • be easy to apply;
  • require no more than two coats;
  • be suitable for the inside and outside of the enclosure;
  • provide a lighter finish than black;
  • allow some wood grain to remain visible.

Edge sealing

The sawn edges of the plywood should be sealed before applying the final finish.

  • Hornbach — PaintPlex edge sealer, 250 g

The edge sealer should first be tested together with the selected stain. Because the stain is transparent, the sealer may remain visible through the final finish.

Preferred stain

  • Hornbach — Hybrid wood stain, silver grey

The stain is transparent and is intended to be applied in two coats on a new finish.

A smaller 375 ml tin can be used to test the complete finishing process on an offcut of the actual Okoumé plywood.

Proposed test

  1. Sand the surface and sawn edges.
  2. Apply edge sealer to the sawn edge.
  3. Apply the first coat of silver-grey stain.
  4. Allow it to dry.
  5. Apply a second coat.
  6. Check the colour, coverage, wood grain and visibility of the edge sealer.

Estimated surface area

The provisional enclosure dimensions are 900 × 450 × 100 mm.

PartsArea per coat
Front and rear panels1.62 m²
Top and bottom panels0.36 m²
Side panels0.18 m²
Total2.16 m²

For two coats this is 4.32 m² without reserve, or about 5.2 m² with a 20% allowance.

The actual area may be lower if part of the front consists of plexiglass or an open display frame.
