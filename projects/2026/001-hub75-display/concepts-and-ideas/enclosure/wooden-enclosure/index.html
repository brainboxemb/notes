Wooden Enclosure Design Considerations

Two enclosure concepts are currently being considered for the HUB75 LED matrix display.

Rear assembly

In this concept, the enclosure is assembled from the rear.

The display panels, electronics and other internal parts are mounted from the back of the enclosure.

Removable top cover

In this concept, the top panel of the enclosure acts as a removable lid.

The plexiglass front and possibly a separate frame containing the LED panels can be inserted into the enclosure from the top.

Design source

Both concepts are based on OpenSCAD models. The source model is preserved under Information / Reference files.

Wood type

Different types and thicknesses of plywood are being considered:

  • unprimed Okoumé plywood;
  • primed Okoumé plywood;
  • Okoumé plywood with paintable foil;
  • 10 mm plywood;
  • 18 mm plywood.
