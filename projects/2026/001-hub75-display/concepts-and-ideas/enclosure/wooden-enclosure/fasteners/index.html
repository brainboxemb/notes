Fasteners for the Wooden Enclosure

An M4 screw-in insert lets us bolt a removable part to the wooden enclosure. The insert is screwed into a blind hole in the plywood, so the bolt can be tightened from the same side, without drilling through the panel.

M4 fasteners

Mounting in the flat face of the plywood

  • Screw-in insert: a blind hole is drilled in the flat surface. The insert and the bolt can be installed from the same side. This seems the more practical option for the intended arrangement, provided the panel is thick enough.
  • T-nut: normally goes in from the back. The bolt then pulls its flange against the wood. Mounting a conventional T-nut on the bolt side does not provide that benefit.

Panel thickness: 10, 12 and 18 mm Okoumé plywood are options. The Hettich insert needs a 6 mm-wide hole; its required depth depends on the insert’s length. Before choosing the plywood thickness, check that the insert fits without drilling through the sheet.
