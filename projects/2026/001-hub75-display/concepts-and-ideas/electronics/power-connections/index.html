HUB75 Power Connections

References and notes about distributing power to HUB75 LED matrix panels.

Standard power cable

  • Adafruit — Replacement 5V Power Cable for RGB LED Matrices
  • Adafruit forum discussion about the cable

HUB75 panels are often supplied with standard power cables, frequently with a split for connecting multiple panels.

Alternative cables are not easy to find. In particular, it appears difficult to find a simple HUB75 power connector with loose wires that can receive a custom termination.

Current approach

The most practical solution currently seems to be reusing the available standard HUB75 power cables and connecting their spade terminals to a barrier terminal block.

  • Amazon.nl — HUAZIZ five-position barrier terminal block
  • YouTube — Busbars versus terminal blocks

The spade terminals at the cable ends appear to be suitable for M3 screws.
