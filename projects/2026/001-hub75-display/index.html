HUB75 LED Matrix Display

● Active · 📐 Design · Started 2026-07 · Updated 2026-10-29

Current: Refining the enclosure, panel mounting and 5 V power-distribution concept for the 5-panel HUB75 display.

HUB75, Electronics, OpenSCAD, Woodworking, 3D Printing

This project is about building a display from five P5 HUB75 LED matrix panels. The five portrait-oriented panels together form a display approximately 800 × 320 mm in size with a total resolution of 160 × 64 pixels.

The project collects design notes, references and possible solutions for the enclosure, panel mounting, electronics and software.

Project goal

The concept image shows the intended five-panel arrangement and its physical size and pixel resolution. It is a project-owned illustration for context, not a final enclosure design.

Visual reference

Image/source: Instructables — https://www.instructables.com/Simple-Extruded-Aluminum-Frame-for-LED-Panels/

This external example is included only as a visual reference for the appearance and scale of a multi-panel HUB75 display. The aluminium extrusion construction shown there is not the current enclosure design.

Current focus

The current work is focused on the enclosure design, the mounting details for the five display panels and the 5 V power-distribution concept.
