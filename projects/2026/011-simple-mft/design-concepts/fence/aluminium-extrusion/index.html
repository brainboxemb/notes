Aluminium Extrusion

Two I-type aluminium extrusion profiles with 5 mm slots are candidates for a simple MFT fence or stop track. Two supplier combinations are documented below: Motedis and Reichelt. The lengths and basket totals differ, so compare complete orders rather than prices per profile alone.

20×20 I-type slot 5 — 800 mm

  • Profile cross-section: 20 × 20 mm.
  • Four open 5 mm slots, with a nominal depth of 6.35 mm.
  • Anodized aluminium.
  • The manufacturer specifies a core suitable for an M5 thread.
  • Candidate for the main fence extrusion.

Product page — Motedis 20×20 I-type slot 5

20×10 I-type slot 5 — 1000 mm

  • Profile cross-section: 20 × 10 mm.
  • One open 5 mm slot, with a nominal depth of 6.35 mm.
  • Anodized aluminium.
  • Candidate for a low-profile guide or stop track.

Option 2 — Reichelt

Reichelt lists two DOLD MECHATRONIK I-type slot-5 profiles. These are both 1000 mm long, unlike the Motedis selection, in which the 20×20 profile is 800 mm long.

Design considerations

  • Determine which section works best as the main fence and which as a movable stop or accessory track.
  • Check compatibility of slot-5 nuts and fittings, especially for a flip stop.
  • Design a repeatable way to position the fence relative to the MFT dog-hole grid and guide rail.
  • The two lengths are candidates, not finalized dimensions of the CAD design.
