Dog Locks

Dog locks and rail clips are small accessories that keep a guide rail positioned against bench dogs. These are reference products for the Simple MFT project; compatibility and performance have not been tested.

UJK MFT Dog Rail Clip (set of 2)

A spring-loaded clip that slides into the rail’s T-slot and connects to a bench dog. Intended for 20 mm dogs and Festool/Makita guide rails.

  • GereedschapPro — UJK MFT Dog Rail Clip
  • UJK — Dog Rail Clip (manufacturer)

Stanton Dog Locks (pair)

Designed to secure a guide rail against 20 mm long bench dogs. The listing describes compatibility with Festool, Makita and Powertec-style guide rails. The V2 uses fasteners including thumb screws so the rail can be released or repositioned. The listing offers optional matching long dogs.

  • ToolTheory on Etsy — Stanton Dog Locks (pair)

Adjustable hinged rail-dog supports (set of 2)

A different type of rail-positioning aid, sold as a pair and advertised as height-adjustable for 19–20 mm dog holes. Unlike simple dog locks, this is a hinged support concept. Dimensions, rail compatibility and actual fit are not independently verified.

  • Temu — Adjustable hinged rail-dog supports (set of 2)

3D-printable MFT Dog Locks (MakerWorld)

A downloadable MFT Dog Locks for Makita/Festool design by WoodworkerMFT, described by its creator as an independent interpretation of Stanton-style dog locks. The listed hardware is four M6 heat-set inserts (5 mm high, 8 mm outer diameter) and four M6 × 12 mm thumbscrews with heads no wider than 19 mm, to avoid colliding with the bench dogs. The creator’s suggested print profile is 0.2 mm layers and 100% infill. This is an interesting printable alternative, but its fit and durability have not been tested here.

  • MakerWorld — MFT Dog Locks for Makita/Festool

The source link is cleaned; the original print-profile fragment and tracking parameters are omitted.

Benchdog Tools Hinge Dogs

These Hinge Dogs are different from small rail-lock clips: they keep a guide rail attached while allowing it to hinge upwards, making it easier to position or remove a workpiece on a compact MFT, including a table close to a wall. Benchdog Tools lists compatibility with Festool, Makita, Milwaukee, Kreg, Excel and Triton guide rails.

  • Available with 65 mm or 95 mm rail dogs. The maker states that the shorter version can accommodate material up to 25 mm thick.
  • Supplied as a pair with mounting hardware; check available clearance beneath the MFT top.
  • The manufacturer warns that an earlier installation video was incorrect; follow its updated fitting instructions.

Source: Benchdog Tools — Hinge Dogs.

Relevance for Simple MFT

The UJK and Stanton products illustrate two methods of holding a rail against dogs. The hinged support illustrates raising and positioning a rail. For the Simple MFT design, check how each works with the chosen rail profile, dog height and cutting direction before selecting a solution.
