Dutch Electronics Shops

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

A practical reference list of Dutch electronics suppliers for maker, embedded, Raspberry Pi, prototyping and workshop projects.

Assortments, stock, shipping terms and opening hours change, so the shop itself remains the source for current details. Shipping figures below were checked on 30 October 2026.

Maker, Raspberry Pi and embedded shops

Kiwi Electronics

Strong Raspberry Pi and prototyping range. Physical pickup/counter in The Hague.

Useful for: Raspberry Pi, HATs, Waveshare displays, Adafruit, SparkFun and Seeed Studio

TinyTronics

Broad maker-electronics catalogue with pickup available in Eindhoven.

Useful for: development boards, components, sensors, displays, power, connectors and tools

Bits & Parts

Hobby electronics, robotics and prototyping parts, including some 3D-printer components.

Useful for: Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32/ESP8266, sensors, LEDs, switches and project parts

Ben's Electronics

Dutch hobby-electronics webshop with stock in Waalwijk. Collection is possible by appointment; it is not a normal walk-in shop.

Useful for: microcontrollers, modules, components, sensors, displays, connectors and prototyping

Elektronica Voor Jou

Maker electronics with pickup available in Leusden.

Useful for: Raspberry Pi, Arduino, educational electronics, components and 3D printing

SOS Solutions

Large Raspberry Pi and SBC-oriented catalogue, especially useful for complete kits and accessories.

Useful for: Raspberry Pi boards, Zero products, HATs, kits, ODROID and home automation

Opencircuit

Broad prototyping and maker-electronics shop; useful for comparing availability of specific boards or displays.

Useful for: modules, components, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, Adafruit, Pololu and Waveshare

RaspberryStore

Specialist Raspberry Pi webshop and official Raspberry Pi Approved Reseller. Webshop only.

Useful for: Raspberry Pi boards, kits, cases, power supplies and official ecosystem accessories

Large component distributors — private-customer friendly

These distributors are useful when you need an exact manufacturer part number, IC, connector, sensor or industrial component rather than a maker-oriented module.

Mouser ElectronicsConsumer

Very large authorised distributor with a Dutch office in Eindhoven. Particularly useful when you already know the exact manufacturer part number.

Private customers: Yes. Mouser accepts normal private-customer orders and supports iDEAL.

Shipping NL: Free on most orders from €75; below that threshold the available shipping charge is shown at checkout.

Useful for: authorised semiconductors, connectors, sensors, dev boards and exact manufacturer part numbers

Also: New customer information

DigiKeyConsumer

Large authorised distributor with an exceptionally broad catalogue. Standard orders normally ship from DigiKey’s US distribution centre.

Private customers: Yes. Private customers can order and pay with normal consumer payment methods.

Shipping NL: €25 below €75; free from €75 for EUR orders to the Netherlands. Marketplace items can have separate shipping.

Useful for: very broad component selection, datasheets, engineering tools and exact part-number sourcing

Also: Delivery time and cost

TMEConsumer

Transfer Multisort Elektronik is a Polish component distributor headquartered in Łódź, where its main logistics operation is based. It also has a Dutch subsidiary in Eindhoven. The relatively low Economy shipping cost makes it practical for smaller component orders.

Private customers: Yes. TME Nederland has explicit consumer terms and delivers to consumers in the Netherlands.

Shipping NL: Standard Economy up to 15 kg: €7.14 incl. VAT. Faster services and heavier parcels cost more.

Useful for: components, connectors, enclosures, automation, tools and many European/industrial brands

Also: Transport methods and costs

Reichelt ElektronikConsumer

German electronics distributor with a catalogue that sits between a component distributor and a general electronics shop.

Private customers: Yes. Reichelt explicitly supports private customers and shows consumer pricing including VAT.

Shipping NL: DPD up to 10 kg: €6.95. DHL or UPS up to 10 kg: €9.95; higher weights cost more.

Useful for: components, Raspberry Pi/Arduino, power, test equipment, workshop and general electronics

Also: Shipping costs

Conrad ElectronicConsumer

Conrad is broader than a pure component distributor, but is still useful for electronics and workshop purchases. Check whether an item is sold directly by Conrad or by a Marketplace partner.

Private customers: Yes for Conrad's own assortment. Conrad Marketplace products are intended for registered business customers.

Shipping NL: €5.95 below €100; free from €100 incl. VAT. Orders up to €15 also get a €2.95 low-order surcharge.

Useful for: general electronics, tools, measurement equipment, components and technical products

Also: Shipping costs

LCSC ElectronicsConsumer

Large international component distributor closely associated with the JLCPCB ecosystem. Particularly useful for low-cost component sourcing and Chinese manufacturers, but shipping/import economics matter more than with EU-based distributors.

Private customers: Yes. There is no minimum order value.

Shipping NL: Calculated from destination, weight and shipping method. Orders below $15 in merchandise value get a $3 handling fee. Import taxes, duties and brokerage can be the customer's responsibility.

Useful for: low-cost electronic components, Chinese manufacturers and parts that fit well with JLCPCB assembly/BOM workflows

Also: Shipping calculation

Pollin ElectronicConsumer

German electronics retailer with a broad and sometimes unusual catalogue, including clearance and surplus-style items. It sits somewhere between Reichelt and a general technical electronics shop.

Private customers: Yes. Consumer prices include VAT, and Pollin ships directly to the Netherlands.

Shipping NL: Flat-rate shipping to the Netherlands: €8.99 incl. VAT for normal parcels; bulky items can carry an additional surcharge.

Useful for: electronics, components, cables, power, tools, surplus/clearance items and general technical products

Also: Shipping and payment

Bürklin ElektronikConsumer

German component distributor with a strong professional catalogue. More specialist than Pollin and more approachable to private customers than the strongly B2B-only distributors, although ordering from outside Germany is a little less frictionless.

Private customers: Yes. Bürklin has explicit consumer terms and ships to the Netherlands. Guest checkout for private customers is only offered in Germany, so Dutch private customers may need an account.

Shipping NL: Netherlands: €10.40 excl. VAT per shipment up to 30 kg.

Useful for: quality electronic components, connectors, electromechanical parts, tools and professional engineering supplies

Also: Shipping information

Large component distributors — B2B-first

These suppliers are still valuable for searching stock, manufacturer part numbers and industrial components, but their ordering model is more business-oriented.

RSB2B-first

RS Components is one of the major industrial distributors. Prices are commonly presented excluding VAT, so it feels more B2B-oriented even though consumer terms exist.

Private customers: Private ordering is legally supported through RS's separate B2C terms, but the webshop and pricing presentation remain strongly business-oriented.

Shipping NL: Current checkout: €5.95; free above €90 excl. VAT.

Useful for: industrial components, electronics, automation, test equipment, tools and RS PRO

Also: B2C terms

FarnellB2B

Excellent technical catalogue and stock reference, but not the obvious choice for a private hobby order because its sales terms are B2B.

Private customers: No for normal private purchases: Farnell's Dutch conditions state that it is a business-to-business distributor and that orders are for business purposes.

Shipping NL: Express next-working-day delivery is currently €14.99. Newark/US-stock items can carry an additional €25 per-order charge. Farnell also currently advertises a €50 minimum order value for standard ordering.

Useful for: electronic components, semiconductors, development tools and engineering parts

Also: Delivery information

General marketplaces

These are not specialist component distributors, but they are often useful for generic electronics, tools, cables, power supplies, enclosures and accessories.

Amazon.nl / Amazon.deMarketplace

Very broad marketplace and increasingly useful for electronics and workshop purchases. Prefer listings sold by known manufacturers or established sellers when exact specifications, traceability or component authenticity matter.

Private customers: Yes.

Shipping NL: Varies by seller, fulfilment method, order value and Prime status; check the order itself for the current delivery charge.

Useful for: tools, cables, adapters, power supplies, enclosures, generic modules and Raspberry Pi accessories

Also: Amazon.de

bol.comMarketplace

Useful for comparing mainstream products and Dutch-market availability. As with Amazon, the actual seller and product brand matter more than the marketplace name when buying technical components.

Private customers: Yes.

Shipping NL: Standard delivery is free from €25. Below €25, orders delivered by bol cost €2.99; partner-supplied orders below €25 normally have no separate delivery charge.

Useful for: tools, workshop equipment, cables, adapters, power supplies, storage and general electronics

Also: Delivery costs

Components, workshop and repair

Vanallesenmeer

Based in Hengelo, with a broad mix of electronic components and maker parts.

Useful for: loose components, connectors, prototyping parts and Arduino/Raspberry Pi/ESP modules

Tessatronic

Traditional electronics shop and webshop at Oude Postweg 184, Hengelo. Some catalogue prices on the site are marked as last updated in 2024, so check current price and availability.

Useful for: loose components, connectors, power supplies, batteries, measurement and repair parts

Eleshop

Electronics-workbench specialist. Pickup is possible in Eindhoven, although it is not a normal walk-in retail shop.

Useful for: soldering equipment, oscilloscopes, measurement equipment and lab tools

Professional and industrial sources

Antratek

More professionally and industrially oriented than most hobby electronics shops; also carries Raspberry Pi products.

Useful for: embedded and industrial electronics, specialist development hardware and modules

Choosing a supplier

For Raspberry Pi boards and official accessories, start with Kiwi Electronics or RaspberryStore. For broad maker parts and modules, compare TinyTronics, Bits & Parts, Ben’s Electronics, Elektronica Voor Jou and Opencircuit. For exact manufacturer part numbers and serious component sourcing as a private customer, TME, Reichelt, Mouser, DigiKey, LCSC and Bürklin are especially useful. Pollin is a good broader electronics alternative. TME, Reichelt and Pollin are generally friendlier for smaller EU orders because their shipping costs are predictable, while Mouser and DigiKey become more attractive above their free-shipping thresholds. LCSC can be very attractive for low-cost components, especially alongside JLCPCB, but international shipping and import handling matter more. RS remains useful but feels more B2B-oriented; Farnell is explicitly B2B. For individual components or a physical electronics counter, Tessatronic and Vanallesenmeer remain useful. For measurement and soldering equipment, Eleshop is worth checking.
