Dutch Electronics Shops

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Reviewed: 30 Oct 2026Current

A practical reference list of Dutch electronics suppliers that are useful for maker, embedded, Raspberry Pi, prototyping and workshop projects.

This is not intended to be a complete directory. Assortments, stock, shipping terms and opening hours change, so the shop itself remains the source for current details.

Maker, Raspberry Pi and embedded shops

Kiwi Electronics

Kiwi Electronics is particularly useful for Raspberry Pi, Arduino, micro:bit, Adafruit, SparkFun, Waveshare, Seeed Studio and prototyping hardware.

  • strong Raspberry Pi and HAT/add-on range;
  • useful source for Waveshare displays;
  • physical pickup/counter in The Hague.

TinyTronics

TinyTronics has a broad maker-electronics range including development boards, components, sensors, displays, power, connectors, tools and 3D-printing parts.

  • broad catalogue rather than only Raspberry Pi;
  • good source for displays and small modules;
  • pickup is possible in Eindhoven.

Bits & Parts

Bits & Parts focuses on hobby electronics and project parts.

  • Arduino, Raspberry Pi, ESP32/ESP8266 and IoT;
  • sensors, components, LEDs, switches and tools;
  • also carries robotics and 3D-printer parts.

Ben’s Electronics

Ben’s Electronics is a Dutch hobby-electronics webshop with stock shipped from its own warehouse in Waalwijk.

  • microcontrollers and modules, including ESP32 and Arduino-compatible boards;
  • components, sensors, displays, connectors and prototyping parts;
  • also carries tools, robotics and other DIY electronics;
  • it is not a normal walk-in shop, but collection is possible by appointment.

Elektronica Voor Jou

Elektronica Voor Jou combines maker electronics with education and 3D printing.

  • Raspberry Pi, Arduino and educational electronics;
  • components, sensors and modules;
  • pickup available in Leusden.

SOS Solutions

SOS Solutions has a large Raspberry Pi and SBC-oriented catalogue.

  • Raspberry Pi boards, Zero products, accessories and HATs;
  • ODROID and home-automation hardware;
  • useful when looking for complete kits and accessories rather than individual components only.

Opencircuit

Opencircuit is a broad prototyping and maker-electronics shop.

  • modules and components;
  • Raspberry Pi, Arduino, Adafruit, Pololu and Waveshare products;
  • useful alternative supplier when comparing availability of a specific board or display.

RaspberryStore

RaspberryStore is a specialist Raspberry Pi webshop and an official Raspberry Pi Approved Reseller.

  • strongly focused on the Raspberry Pi ecosystem;
  • boards, kits, cases, power supplies and expansions;
  • webshop only; no normal physical shop.

Components, workshop and repair

Vanallesenmeer

Vanallesenmeer is based in Hengelo and carries a wide range of electronic components and maker parts.

  • loose components, connectors and prototyping parts;
  • Arduino, Raspberry Pi and ESP-related modules;
  • brands and product groups including Adafruit, Pololu and SparkFun.

Tessatronic

Tessatronic is a traditional electronics shop and webshop at Oude Postweg 184, Hengelo.

  • loose electronic components;
  • connectors, power supplies, batteries and measurement equipment;
  • communication, light/audio and repair-related parts;
  • useful because it is also a real physical component shop rather than only a webshop.

The website currently notes that some catalogue prices were last updated in 2024, so verify price and availability before relying on an online listing.

Eleshop

Eleshop is especially useful for the electronics workbench.

  • soldering equipment;
  • oscilloscopes, measurement equipment and lab tools;
  • electronics workshop equipment and accessories;
  • pickup is possible in Eindhoven, although it is not a normal walk-in retail shop.

Professional and industrial sources

Antratek

Antratek Electronics is more professionally and industrially oriented than many hobby shops.

  • embedded and industrial electronics;
  • development hardware and specialist modules;
  • also carries Raspberry Pi products.

Choosing a supplier

For Raspberry Pi boards and official accessories, start with Kiwi Electronics or RaspberryStore. For broad maker parts and modules, TinyTronics, Bits & Parts, Ben’s Electronics, Elektronica Voor Jou and Opencircuit are useful comparisons. For individual components or a physical electronics counter, Tessatronic and Vanallesenmeer are especially relevant. For measurement and soldering equipment, Eleshop is worth checking.
