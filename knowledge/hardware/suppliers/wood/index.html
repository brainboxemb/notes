Wood Suppliers

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

A practical list of wood suppliers in the eastern and northern Netherlands, roughly around the Enschede–Arnhem–Zwolle–Drachten area.

The emphasis is on suppliers that are useful for DIY, furniture making, workshop projects and smaller quantities — not just large-volume construction timber.

Fine wood and smaller projects

Arnhemse FijnhouthandelFine wood

Arnhem — Driepoortenweg 12. A specialist fine-wood supplier with a very broad range of solid wood, veneer and furniture-grade sheet material. Particularly interesting for furniture making, instrument building and other projects where species and quality matter.

A useful feature for smaller projects is that several sheet materials are sold as quarter, half and full sheets instead of requiring a full board. For example, Poplar plywood is available in full sheets, half sheets around 125 × 122 cm and quarter sheets around 125 × 61 cm.

Useful for: solid fine woods, veneer, furniture-grade sheet material and smaller project quantities

Also: Webshop

Masave FijnhouthandelSmall quantities

Zwolle — Marconistraat 2A. Particularly well suited to hobbyists and small projects: Masave explicitly specialises in small quantities and sells part of a plank or beam rather than forcing customers to buy trade-sized quantities.

They can also saw, plane and glue wood on site. Walk-in opening hours are limited, so checking the current hours or arranging an appointment is sensible before making a longer trip.

Useful for: small quantities of fine wood, single pieces, furniture projects and on-site sawing, planing and gluing

Houthandel van DeuverenMixed

Dedemsvaart — Galileïstraat 29. A broad timber merchant useful for both normal construction/garden wood and more furniture-oriented material such as oak, walnut, solid panels and fine wood. Private customers are explicitly welcome.

Especially useful for smaller workshop projects: Van Deuveren stocks many sheet materials as quarter sheets around 61 × 122 cm and half sheets around 61 × 244 cm, in addition to full sheets. They also offer cutting to size.

Useful for: furniture oak, fine wood, sheet material, interior projects, construction and garden timber

Also: Quarter sheets

EikenDirectOak

Drachten — Het Helmhout 69. A useful specialist when a project specifically calls for oak. The shop carries oak boards and other timber directly from stock and is open to visiting customers.

Useful for: oak boards, cladding, construction timber and oak for furniture/interior projects

General timber and sheet material

Houtcentre De Bruin

Enschede — Hendrik ter Kuilestraat 175A. Established timber and building-material supplier for East Netherlands. Although strongly oriented toward construction and professional customers, it explicitly also supplies private customers.

Useful for: construction timber, sheet material and general timber for DIY and building projects

De Houtboer

Drachten — De Giek 21–27. Broad timber merchant and building shop with a large stock of wood, sheet materials and hardware. Orders can be collected in Drachten and larger orders can be cut to size.

Useful for: construction timber, sheet material, garden timber, hardware and general project wood

Hout- en Plaathandel Beldman

Zwolle — Voltastraat 6. Timber and sheet-material specialist serving both professional and private customers. Useful as a more traditional houthandel alternative in Zwolle when fine wood is not the main requirement.

Useful for: timber and sheet material for building, renovation, furniture and general DIY projects

Houthandel Scharenborg

Boekelo / Enschede — Ganzebosweg 335. Local timber supplier with sawing, planing and machining services. Worth keeping in mind when standard DIY-store dimensions are not sufficient or material needs to be machined to size.

Useful for: construction timber, sheet material, garden timber, non-standard dimensions and machining

Choosing where to go

For fine wood, furniture wood and unusual species, Arnhemse Fijnhouthandel is the strongest specialist in this group. For small quantities and processing, Masave in Zwolle is especially attractive.

For projects using sheet material in manageable sizes, Van Deuveren and Arnhemse Fijnhouthandel stand out because they sell a range of quarter and half sheets rather than only full commercial boards.

For general construction, workshop or garden timber, Houtcentre De Bruin, De Houtboer, Beldman and Scharenborg are more conventional timber merchants. For oak specifically, EikenDirect is worth checking.
