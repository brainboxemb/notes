E-paper Displays

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

E-paper displays are useful when low power consumption and readability without a backlight matter more than update speed. The modules below look similar, but differ substantially in colour support, refresh behaviour and board features.

2.13-inch Waveshare HAT comparison

These four modules look very similar, but the display technology and controller-board features differ significantly.

ModelColoursResolutionPartial refreshKey point

The biggest practical distinction is therefore not the physical screen size but colour, refresh behaviour and board revision. For a frequently updated status display, the black/white versions are much more suitable than the colour versions.

Waveshare 2.13-inch e-Paper HAT V4.x — black/white

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Waveshare; SKU 12915.
  • Display: 2.13 inches, black/white, 250 × 122 pixels.
  • Interface: SPI.
  • Board size: approximately 65 × 30.2 mm.
  • Refresh: around 2 seconds full refresh and 0.3 seconds partial refresh.
  • Main advantage: simple, fast e-paper option when colour is not needed.

Waveshare 2.13-inch e-Paper HAT (G) — four colours

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Waveshare; SKU 24908.
  • Display: 2.13 inches, red/yellow/black/white, 250 × 122 pixels.
  • Interface: 3-wire or 4-wire SPI.
  • Board size: approximately 65 × 30.2 mm.
  • Refresh: no partial refresh. Waveshare’s own pages are inconsistent: the product page lists 25 seconds, while a newer selection table on the HAT+ page lists 16 seconds. Kiwi also lists 25 seconds. For planning purposes, treat it as a slow full-refresh colour panel.
  • Main advantage: four distinct colours without a backlight.
  • Trade-off: much slower updates than the black/white model.

Links

  • Manufacturer / documentation: Waveshare product page
  • Where to buy: Kiwi Electronics (NL) · Opencircuit (NL) · Waveshare

Waveshare 2.13-inch e-Paper HAT (B) — three colours and a revision trap

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Waveshare; SKU 13448.
  • Colours: red, black and white.
  • Refresh: around 15 seconds; no partial refresh.
  • Important revision difference: the supplied Kiwi page still describes a 212 × 104 version. Waveshare’s current product page and TinyTronics’ V4 listing use the same 13448 product number but specify 250 × 122.
  • Practical consequence: do not use the product number alone to determine the panel resolution. Check whether the actual board/display is an older V3-era unit or the current V4 before choosing a driver, image dimensions or enclosure.

This is the clearest example of why these nearly identical HATs need to be documented by revision as well as SKU.

Waveshare 2.13-inch e-Paper HAT+ — black/white with RTC

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Waveshare; SKU 27467.
  • Display: 2.13 inches, black/white, 250 × 122 pixels.
  • Interface: 3-wire or 4-wire SPI, 3.3 V.
  • Refresh: around 2 seconds full, 1.8 seconds quick and 0.3 seconds partial refresh.
  • Board size: approximately 65 × 30.5 mm.
  • RTC: onboard DS3231 real-time clock.
  • Additional board features: onboard EEPROM and a 2-pin JST connector for a 3 V/3.3 V rechargeable lithium backup battery.
  • Mechanical/electrical format: Raspberry Pi HAT+ standard.

Why it matters

For a small Raspberry Pi that may be offline or powered down between uses, the RTC is a meaningful difference: the board can keep time independently and Linux can synchronise its system clock from the DS3231 when no network time source is available.

Waveshare 2.7-inch e-Paper HAT — black/white

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Waveshare; product/SKU 13354 (also WS-13354).
  • Interface: SPI via the Raspberry Pi 40-pin GPIO header.
  • Display: 2.7 inches, 264 × 176 pixels; black/white with four grey levels.
  • Extra: four onboard buttons (KEY1–KEY4).
  • PCB dimensions: 85 × 56 mm, according to Waveshare.
  • Refresh: full update around 6 seconds; partial update around 0.3 seconds on supported revisions.

Waveshare 2.7-inch e-Paper HAT (B) — three colours

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Waveshare; product/SKU 13357.
  • Interface: SPI via the Raspberry Pi 40-pin GPIO header.
  • Display: 2.7 inches, 264 × 176 pixels; red, black and white.
  • Refresh: full update around 15 seconds; no partial-refresh support listed.
  • PCB dimensions: Waveshare lists 73.5 × 56 mm. Check the exact hardware revision and mechanical drawing before designing a holder.

Use case

The red accent is useful for labels and status information, but the much slower full update makes this version less suitable for frequently changing content.

Notes

  • These HATs use SPI rather than HDMI; software needs a compatible driver for the specific panel and hardware revision.
  • Check hardware revision, official pinout and mounting drawing before designing an enclosure or combining a HAT with other GPIO accessories.
