LCD Displays

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

LCD and IPS displays are the better fit when the screen changes frequently or the Raspberry Pi needs a responsive, full-colour user interface.

Pimoroni Display HAT Mini — 2-inch colour IPS LCD

Specifications

  • Manufacturer: Pimoroni; product/SKU PIM589.
  • Display: 2.0-inch IPS LCD, 320 × 240 pixels, up to 65K colours (18-bit-capable panel).
  • Interface: SPI via the Raspberry Pi 40-pin GPIO header; ST7789V2 display controller.
  • Controls: four tactile buttons (A, B, X, Y) and an RGB status LED.
  • Expansion: Qw/ST (Qwiic / STEMMA QT) connector and Breakout Garden / I2C header.
  • PCB dimensions: approximately 65.5 × 35 × 9 mm including header and display.
  • Mounting: fully assembled with pre-soldered socket header; includes two 10 mm standoffs. Fits a Raspberry Pi Zero well, though it extends beyond the Pi Zero outline.

Use case

Unlike the e-paper modules above, this is a continuously powered colour LCD, suitable for frequently changing information and button-driven interfaces. The built-in buttons could be useful for a compact Pi Zero setup; check the mechanical placement before using them with other button accessories.
