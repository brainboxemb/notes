Useful Websites

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

A curated list of websites worth checking for embedded systems, electronics, Raspberry Pi, Linux, maker projects and related hardware developments.

This is intentionally selective rather than a complete directory. Some sources work well as regular news feeds; others are better for occasional browsing, project inspiration or deeper technical articles.

Already tracked in Daily

These sources already have their own tracked source feed in the Daily section.

HackadayDaily

Hardware hacking, electronics, reverse engineering, unusual builds and technical projects.

Useful for: inventive projects, hardware hacking, reverse engineering and unusual techniques

CNX SoftwareDaily

Embedded systems news covering SBCs, microcontrollers, IoT, Linux, processors and open-source hardware.

Useful for: new boards, SoCs, embedded Linux products and detailed hardware announcements

TweakersDaily

Broad Dutch technology news, reviews and product developments.

Useful for: Dutch-market context, mainstream hardware and larger technology developments

Embedded systems and hardware news

LinuxGizmos

Embedded Linux, SBCs, industrial computers, modules and Linux-capable hardware.

Useful for: Linux-oriented boards, industrial computers and embedded devices

Hackster News

Hardware and embedded news with a strong maker angle. The direct News page is a cleaner entry point than the general Hackster homepage.

Useful for: microcontrollers, edge AI, unusual hardware projects and product announcements

Seeed Studio Blog

Seeed product developments, embedded AI, IoT, sensing and project examples.

Useful for: XIAO, SenseCAP, edge AI, sensing and Seeed ecosystem developments

Also: Seeed News Center

OpenELAB Blog

Product-oriented articles, tutorials and comparisons around maker and embedded hardware.

Useful for: practical comparisons and modules from Waveshare, Seeed, LilyGO, M5Stack and Elecrow

Electronics-Lab

Embedded hardware news, electronic projects and circuit-oriented articles.

Useful for: a mix of new boards/modules and more traditional electronics projects

All About Circuits

Electronics engineering news with more emphasis on components, semiconductors, sensors and engineering technology.

Useful for: MCUs, ICs, sensors, interfaces and semiconductor technology

Projects and maker inspiration

Hackster Projects

Community hardware projects with filtering by platform, difficulty and subject.

Useful for: concrete ESP32, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, display, sensor, robotics and IoT implementations

Electromaker

Electronics news, reviews and maker content. The project browser is particularly useful for filtering by platform and subject.

Useful for: practical builds and visual references

Also: Electromaker Projects

Adafruit Blog

Maker electronics, open-source hardware, CircuitPython, tutorials and community projects.

Useful for: small electronics projects, displays, LEDs, sensors, CircuitPython and unusual maker ideas

Raspberry Pi News

Official Raspberry Pi announcements, product updates and selected community projects.

Useful for: primary-source Raspberry Pi announcements and ecosystem developments

Linux, systems and homelab

Jeff Geerling

Raspberry Pi, SBCs, networking, homelab hardware, Linux and practical hardware experiments.

Useful for: hands-on Raspberry Pi, SBC, networking and homelab testing

ServeTheHome

Servers, networking, storage, mini PCs and homelab hardware.

Useful for: mini PCs, network hardware, storage and higher-end small systems

Phoronix

Linux hardware support, kernel developments, open-source graphics and performance benchmarking.

Useful for: Linux kernel hardware enablement, drivers, graphics and performance

Electronics engineering and community

EEVblog

Dave Jones’ electronics engineering content and long-running electronics community. The forum is often the better entry point for detailed technical discussions.

Useful for: electronics engineering, measurement equipment and detailed community knowledge

Also: EEVblog Forum

Possible additions to Daily tracking

The strongest candidates if more sites are added as tracked Daily sources are LinuxGizmos, Hackster News, Raspberry Pi News, Jeff Geerling and All About Circuits.

Vendor-oriented sources such as Seeed Studio and OpenELAB are probably better kept as browsing/reference sources unless their signal-to-marketing ratio proves useful enough for regular tracking.
