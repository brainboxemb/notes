Veritas Worksurface

The Veritas Worksurface is a small, portable MFT-style work surface intended for workholding on an existing table or bench rather than a full-size freestanding MFT.

Construction and features

  • Portable platform made from 1½-inch Baltic birch plywood.
  • ¾-inch (19.05 mm) dog holes rather than nominal 20 mm MFT holes: check accessory compatibility.
  • Perimeter aluminium tracks with ¼-20 T-slots for additional clamping options.
  • Rubber feet raise the surface and help keep it in place; optional taller risers are available.
  • Three sizes: small 12 × 15½ in, medium 15½ × 22½ in, and large 19 × 29½ in.

The combination of dog holes, perimeter slots and a compact raised platform makes this an interesting alternative to a full-size MFT. In particular, the perimeter tracks are worth considering for positioning stops or attaching small fixtures. The product is a reference, not a chosen component or a verified design for the Simple MFT project.
