Electrical Suppliers

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

A practical reference list for consumer-friendly electrical installation suppliers in Twente: group/distribution boards, switches and sockets, cable, conduit, installation material, lighting and related tools.

This category is deliberately separate from Electronics suppliers, which are more focused on electronic components, development boards and embedded hardware.

Specialist electrical suppliers

Sander Vunderink ElektromaterialenConsumer

Hengelo — Grindstraat 7. A strong local option for normal electrical-installation material, with a large webshop and stock-oriented assortment. Particularly useful for price-comparing common brands and complete project orders.

Private customers: Yes. Private customers can order directly.

Useful for: group/distribution boards, switches and sockets, wire, cable, submeters, installation material, lighting and tools

ElektramatConsumer

Enschede — Het Poolman 15. One of the largest and most convenient local webshops for electrical-installation materials. Orders can be collected at the Enschede pickup point, but it is not a normal walk-in retail shop: the order must first be placed and confirmed for pickup.

Private customers: Yes. The webshop explicitly serves both installers and people doing their own electrical installation.

Useful for: group/distribution boards, switching material, installation cable, ground cable, conduit, lighting, charging and general electrical installation materials

Also: Contact and pickup

Broader consumer options

These are less specialised than the two suppliers above, but can still be useful when you want to buy installation material locally as a private customer.

WarmteserviceConsumer

Several branches are available in the region, including Hengelo, Almelo and Enschede. Useful when an electrical job overlaps with heating, ventilation, sanitary or other home-installation work and you want a real counter with practical advice.

Private customers: Yes. Private DIY customers are welcome.

Useful for: technical installation materials, including electrical products alongside heating, climate and sanitary

Choosing a supplier

For a normal private electrical project, Sander Vunderink and Elektramat are the most relevant specialist starting points in Twente.

Use Warmteservice when the job crosses into heating, ventilation, sanitary or other home-installation work. For general walk-in DIY materials, see DIY Stores.
