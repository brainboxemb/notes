DIY Stores

Created: 30 Oct 2026 Updated: 30 Oct 2026 Checked: 30 Oct 2026Current

A practical list of consumer DIY / home-improvement stores around Twente and across the nearby German border.

The focus is not on listing every ordinary branch. These are stores that are useful because of their size, assortment, services or because they make an interesting cross-border shopping option.

Twente

HORNBACH EnschedeLarge store

Enschede — Zuiderval 175. Large project-oriented DIY store with garden centre and a major Drive In. In October 2026 HORNBACH opened a roughly 6,000 m² covered Drive In, described by the company as its largest covered Drive In in the Netherlands.

Useful for: building materials, timber, tools, electrical, plumbing, garden and bulk project supplies

BAUHAUS TwenteLarge store

Hengelo — Het Plein 10. Large-format DIY store at Plein Westermaat. Useful as the main local alternative to HORNBACH when comparing project materials, tools and general workshop supplies.

Useful for: building materials, timber, tools, hardware, electrical, plumbing, garden and workshop supplies

Just across the German border

There are already several useful consumer DIY stores before you get anywhere near Münster.

HELLWEG GronauCheck status

Gronau — Maybachstraße 2. One of the closest German full-size DIY stores from Enschede.

Current note (30 October 2026): HELLWEG currently shows chain-wide clearance and insolvency information on its website. The Gronau branch is still listed, but check current status before making a special trip.

Useful for: general DIY, building, garden, tools and project supplies

hagebaucentrum Gronau-EpeLarge store

Gronau-Epe — Gronauer Straße 178. A substantial hagebaucentrum with around 9,671 m² of sales area, covered outdoor storage and broad stock for both professional and DIY / Heimwerker projects. This is one of the most relevant full-size German alternatives close to Enschede.

Useful for: building materials, timber, tools, renovation supplies and project materials for professional and DIY customers

Sonderpreis Baumarkt GronauDiscount DIY

Gronau — Heerweg 66. Smaller than a classic full-range Baumarkt, but particularly interesting for screws, nuts and other fasteners sold by weight, plus budget tools and normal renovation supplies.

Useful for: loose fasteners by weight, hand and power tools, paint, renovation, garden and household project supplies

Bauzentrum GevelerRegional

Gronau — Ochtruper Straße 214–216. A regional building-materials specialist with an attached DIY/fachmarkt range. Useful when the project is more construction-material oriented than a normal consumer Baumarkt visit.

Useful for: building materials, timber and practical DIY/building supplies

HELLWEG AhausCheck status

Ahaus — Düwing Dyk 1. Full-size DIY store south of Gronau.

Current note (30 October 2026): as with Gronau, check the current branch status because HELLWEG is presently advertising chain-wide clearance.

Useful for: general DIY, building, garden, tools and project supplies

Sonderpreis Baumarkt AhausDiscount DIY

Ahaus — Hamalandstraße 69–73. A useful second consumer DIY option in Ahaus. The chain is especially interesting for small quantities of screws, nuts and other hardware sold by weight.

Useful for: fasteners by weight, tools, paint, renovation, garden and general DIY supplies

Bettings MühleDifferent

Ahaus — Bahnhofstraße 88. Not a classic big-box Baumarkt, which is exactly why it is interesting. A long-established regional shop with a broad tool, hardware and garden range that complements the normal chains.

Useful for: tools, ironmongery, fasteners, garden equipment, grills and broader household/project supplies

hagebaumarkt OchtrupGermany

Ochtrup — Deipenbrook 1. Another proper consumer DIY store close to the border, useful on the route between Gronau and Rheine.

Useful for: general DIY, building, timber, tools, renovation and garden supplies

Practical notes for Germany

German stores are normally closed on Sundays, unlike several large Dutch DIY stores. Check current branch hours and stock before making a longer cross-border trip.

For an ordinary local purchase, HORNBACH Enschede and BAUHAUS Twente are the obvious starting points. The German stores become more interesting when comparing a specific product, brand, tool or project price — or when the trip can be combined with other shopping.
