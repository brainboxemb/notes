Elektor — FNIRSI DLA-16/32 Plus Logic Analyzer

Added: 30 October 2026.

Interesting as a lower-cost logic-analyzer option to investigate alongside Saleae.

Elektor currently lists two versions:

  • DLA-16 Plus: 16 digital channels;
  • DLA-32 Plus: 32 digital channels.

Shared features include:

  • sampling up to 1 GSa/s;
  • 4 Gbit internal memory;
  • USB 3.0 / USB-C;
  • Buffer, Stream and Roll acquisition modes;
  • 120+ protocol decoders and support for custom protocols;
  • four 3.3 V PWM outputs from 1 Hz to 20 MHz;
  • CSV and BIN export.

When all channels are active, Elektor specifies up to 500 MSa/s for 16 channels on the DLA-16 Plus and 250 MSa/s for 32 channels on the DLA-32 Plus.

At the time this was added, Elektor showed the series as pre-order at approximately €159.95–€239.00, depending on version/configuration.

Note: The price makes this an interesting option alongside Saleae-class logic analyzers, but it is not automatically a feature-for-feature replacement. Current Saleae Logic devices also support analog acquisition on their input channels, while the FNIRSI DLA listing describes digital inputs. Software quality, decoder usability, long-capture behaviour and overall hardware quality are also useful points to keep in mind when comparing them.
