brainboxemb

brainboxemb grew from the idea of a brainbox: a place to capture, structure and keep technical ideas, references and lessons that would otherwise live only in my head. The emb part reflects one of my main interests: embedded electronics and software.

That includes microcontrollers, displays, protocols, Raspberry Pi, hardware/software integration and the tooling around them. From there, the site naturally branches into 3D printing, CAD, workshop and DIY projects, tools, AI and other technical experiments.

Projects document things I am actually building, the Knowledge Base keeps durable subject notes and references, and Daily is for interesting technical material worth saving.
